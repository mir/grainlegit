Grainlegit OSOR Phase II for Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (SM8850)

These QNN contexts and ONNX wrappers are modified model files. Grainlegit
fused the OSOR Phase II adapter into SDXL Inpainting, fixed the inference shape
at 512 × 512, and compiled the resulting pipeline for the Qualcomm Hexagon NPU.

OSOR: One-Step Diffusion Inpainting for Effect-Aware Object Removal
Qinming Zhou, Chenxi Sun, Deyang Kong, Junhao He, Xiangheng Tang, Peike Yu,
Haotian Wu, Leilei Cao, and Linfeng Zhang.
